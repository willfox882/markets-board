## M3 audit fixes
Independent audit of 81a05e6. Each claim was checked in code; all were valid. Regression tests are in `FeedParserTest`, `DedupeRelevanceTest`, `NewsRepositoryTest`, `NewsPollerTest`, `FeedStoreTest`, `NewsUiTest`, `BriefingTest`, `PlainTextAndDatesTest`, `NewsRenderTest`.
1. **The Android XML path was untested** (everything ran on Xerces). `FeedParser` now uses `org.xmlpull.v1.XmlPullParser`: the platform parser on a phone, kxml2 (catalog `kxml2` 2.3.0) in `:sandbox` and as `testImplementation` in `:app`. A test asserts the parser class really is kxml and parses (the real kxml2 classes win over the android.jar stubs). The SAX path is gone. DOCDECL processing is off, nothing is fetched, only the five predefined entities and numeric references reach the parser (`XmlText` rewrites the rest). Namespaces are not processed: elements are matched by written prefix (`dc:date`, `content:encoded`, a prefixed Atom root), which also survives undeclared prefixes. kxml reports a truncated document as a normal end, so an element left open at the end is now an error (partial salvage keeps the finished items). `hasDoctype` is one linear scan of the whole capped input that skips only comments, CDATA and processing instructions (no 100 000 character bound); tests: a 200 KB comment before a DOCTYPE and billion laughs behind a 300 KB comment and a PI.
2. **Dedupe.** Title and summary rules only join items of different feeds (the feeds already merged into the kept item are tracked) published within 12 h; exact-title merge needs the window too; titles that differ by an up word versus a down word ("rise" vs "fall", gain/loss, higher/lower, jump/drop, surge/slump and more) never merge. `cid`, `src`, `source`, `campaign`, `output`, `partner` are no longer stripped; `mod` and `tsrc` are. Same URL always merges.
3. **Cancelled passes and dropped triggers.** `lastAttemptMono` is recorded only when an attempt finishes, so a pass cut short by a pause leaves its feeds due. `tryLock` no longer drops callers: a request during a running pass is merged into one follow-up pass (force ORs, only-failed ANDs) that runs when the current one ends; a cancelled pass hands its request back. A symbol added, the network returning or a resume during a pass therefore still fetches.
4. **Hotspot recovery.** `ConnectivityNetworkSignal` also fires from `onCapabilitiesChanged` when `NET_CAPABILITY_VALIDATED` turns true (once per network; Markets uses the same class). Timeout and Network failures never back off beyond 10 min (one poll period); 429 and HTTP errors still grow to 30 min.
5. **Wrong boot clock.** A cache written by a clock ahead of the device has an unknown age: the list is `Stale(ageKnown = false)` ("Not up to date"), never "Updated just now". Fresh now requires a success in this run (`confirmed`); a failed pass no longer turns a recent cache Fresh.
6. **Server date.** The offset is the median over feeds (one bad server cannot move it), needs more than 5 min of skew, and accounts for `Age`. The "now" used for dates is the corrected device clock. Feed dates more than 5 min ahead (was 1 day) are replaced by the fetch time.
7. **Banner cause.** `NewsState.errorKind` is the failure most feeds share (clock/TLS first on ties); the banner says offline, slow, busy, check the date and time, unreadable or a server problem instead of always "Can't reach".
8. **Cleartext and browser.** Feed addresses must be https (add refuses `http://` in any case; a stored http feed is dropped on load). `LocalOpenUrl` now returns Boolean, the article screen shows an amber "No browser on this device" when it fails, the scheme check ignores case everywhere (`FeedParser.safeUrl`, `MainActivity.openUrl`).
9. **Briefing.** Headlines only from the last 24 h and not in `alreadySpoken`; `Briefing.headlineIds` returns what was read; "No new headlines." when none; "Headlines may be out of date." with `headlinesFromCache`; clause or " as "/" after " cuts and no dangling filler words; speaking time is estimated (digits and spelled letters cost more) with a 42 s and 110 word cap; negative prices say "minus"; "C$1.2B" becomes "1.2 billion Canadian dollars", "%" becomes "percent"; an instrument without a price says "No price available for X."; the currency is spoken only when it is not Canadian dollars ("X E Q T, 41 dollars 25 cents, down 4.6 percent", "Microsoft, 414 dollars 60 cents U S, ..."); FX says "The U S dollar buys 1 dollar 37 Canadian". Ages come from `RowStatus.Stale/Error.ageMs` (new structured field, filled by `UiMapper`), not from regexes over display text.
10. **Low.** The first-word name rule is skipped for funds, so "iShares ..." alone no longer tags every story as XEQT (the ticker or "iShares Core Equity" does). `PlainText` removes U+200E/F and the bidi embeddings, overrides and isolates (U+202A-202E, U+2066-2069).
11. **UI.** The amber status line and banner are ONE line with Retry when stale ("Can't reach the news sources  ·  40 min ago"). All | My symbols is a light text toggle (`TextToggle`, white underline on the hairline) so the first headline starts higher. Open article is a secondary pill.
12. **Feeds.** Financial Post (`https://financialpost.com/feed`) is a default (best effort; an existing `feeds.json` keeps its own list, use reset to get it).
Disagreements: none of substance. Two notes: (a) `Fresh` also needs an actual success in this run, which makes a just-restarted app show Stale (with the cache age) until the first pass finishes, a second or two; that matches the SWR intent. (b) Feed dates in the future by up to 5 min are kept as given; anything later becomes "now" and `dateEstimated`.

## M3 decisions and judgement calls
- **DOCTYPE is refused, not stripped.** Old RSS 0.91 feeds with a Netscape DTD reference therefore fail with a Parse error. Safer than trying to sanitise a prolog; every default feed is RSS 2.0 or Atom.
- **Pull parser (was SAX, changed in the audit)**: `javax.xml.stream` does not exist on Android and the SAX implementation differs from the desktop one, `org.xmlpull` is the same KXmlParser family on both, and a pull loop gives partial salvage for free. The parser decodes bytes itself (BOM, declared encoding, HTTP charset) and hands the parser a character stream, so the declared encoding can never disagree with the decoder. `HttpResponse` gained an optional `bytes` field for that (the real fetcher fills it; fakes may leave it null and the parser uses `body`).
- **Per-symbol feed tags**: items of a per-symbol Yahoo feed are tagged with that symbol only when their text names nothing on the watchlist. Tagging every item of such a feed made "My symbols" noisy (a Microsoft story arriving through the XEQT feed was tagged XEQT too).
- **Fresh vs Stale**: 25 min without a success. Offline with old headlines is shown as Stale plus the amber "Can't reach the news sources" band (not as an error, the headlines are still useful); Error is only for "nothing to show and everything failed".
- **Dedupe thresholds** (Dice 0.8, at least 4 tokens each, 36 h window; 0.5 when the 40+ character summaries are identical; exact normalised titles and canonical URLs always) are cheap guesses tuned on the fixtures. False merges lose a headline, false splits show a near-duplicate; I chose to risk the second.
- **Dateless items** are timed at first sight, so they sort as new when first seen and age out normally; the article screen shows no published time for them.
- **Em and en dashes in feed text become hyphens** (" - " and "-"), so the app never displays an em-dash even when a publisher uses one.
- **Manual Refresh ignores backoff** (the owner pressed it), the automatic poll does not. A pass already running makes a second request return at once (the header already says "Refreshing").
- **Briefing wording** is mine (the owner's two examples are reproduced exactly): "Here is your market briefing.", "Top headlines.", "Prices may be delayed", "You appear to be offline, so these prices may be old", "Markets are closed.". Easy to change in `Briefings`.
- **Glance news** shows the newest 3 items (not "most relevant"), with their age. M4 decides how glance is triggered.
- The News UI keeps `LazyColumn` rows unkeyed (same reason as Markets: a duplicate key would crash).

## M2 audit fixes
Each claim was checked in code; all were valid. Regression tests are in `MarketsRenderTest`, `RefreshControllerTest`, `StalenessTest`, `MarketsPresenterTest`, `UiMapperTest`, `CoreTextsTest`.
1. **Overlays block touches.** The list is no longer composed while the detail or add sheet is open (its `LazyListState` is hoisted, scroll position survives), and both overlay roots swallow leftover pointer events in the Main pass. Note: consuming in the Initial pass broke the overlay's own buttons (found by the new pointer test), hence Main. Pointer tests tap a grid over the detail and add overlays (with edit mode underneath) and assert nothing reaches the list while the overlay's own buttons still work.
2. **Suspend and resume.** `AndroidClock` (monotonic = `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime`, includes sleep) is injected in the Application. The controller records the pause time; on resume it recomputes ages first, ages the quotes by (wall gap minus monotonic gap) when that exceeds 5 s (`QuotesSource.ageShift`, also shortens cooldowns) and, when the absence exceeded 60 s, clears the per-symbol history so every symbol is refreshed at once. Coroutine `delay` does not run in deep sleep, but the loop restarts on resume anyway.
3. **Market-aware staleness.** The 1D stale limit is max(5 min, 2.5 x the market phase's nominal poll interval): 5 min REGULAR, 12.5 min PRE/POST, 75 min closed. A closed-market quote no longer flips to Stale five minutes after each fetch and the header no longer says "Delayed" for it.
4. **Search duplicates.** `parseSearch` and the presenter both `distinctBy` symbol.
5. **Done / IME.** Symbol-shaped text (capitals, digits, `. ^ = -`) is tried as a ticker, preferring a result with exactly that symbol. Anything else (spaces, lowercase) picks the first result of the CURRENT search (results remember which text they answer), or shows a hint ("Still searching..." / "Pick a result ... or type the full symbol in capital letters"); a guessed ticker is never added. The typed text is never overwritten when an add finishes (cleared only if unchanged). Out-of-order answers are ignored (tested with a non-cancellable late search).
6. **Polling cost and network.** `PollPolicy`: REGULAR and unknown 60 s, PRE/POST 5 min (changed from 60 s), closed 30 min or the next session start when sooner. `NetworkSignal` (Android: `ConnectivityNetworkSignal`, default-network callback) triggers an immediate pass for symbols with no quote or a failed refresh, ignoring the minimum gap, still honouring cooldown and pause. The controller restarts only when the SET of symbols changes (pure reorder: no restart, timer unchanged). Manual Refresh and Retry are debounced to one per 5 s (per key).
7. **Detail.** Tab bar hidden while detail or add is open; chart has High/Low of the range, a start label and an "as of ..." end label (chart time, exchange zone); a held range that failed to refresh keeps its chart and shows an amber note with "Try again"; "Previous close" is "Prev. session close"; loading and error chart areas are compact (110 dp).
8. **Mapping.** Unknown time zone: the zone label is omitted and the time is shown in the device zone (no invented "UTC"). The header "Refresh" keeps its slot and only dims while refreshing.
9. **Design.** Row reflowed: name up to two lines (full width) with the price right, `SYMBOL  ·  EXCHANGE` with the change right, full-width 26 dp sparkline strip with the dashed baseline, about 112 dp tall; no minimum price width. Edit mode: Rename, Replace, Remove are three separate 48 dp bordered buttons with full-contrast text; arrows carry "Move XEQT.TO up/down" descriptions. The error status band is the reason (up to two lines), then "Last price 9 min ago", with Retry in its own column. With the clock-skew banner showing, per-row stale bands are suppressed (error rows keep theirs).
10. **M3/M4 preparation.** `ActiveGate` (shared; used by `AppRuntime` and the sandbox, reusable by a news poller), `UiMode` (Normal, Glance) combined into `MarketsUiState.mode` through an injected flow; `MarketsBoardRoot(state, news, initialTab, actions, mode)`. ## M2 decisions and judgement calls
- **Green "Live" dot**: the brief allows a green dot for fresh data, so `liveDotColor()` (Components.kt) is the one non-price use of green; amber for stale/offline, grey for closed/loading. RepoHygieneTest was updated to allow exactly that one extra line.
- **Header "Updated"** uses the newest provider time among held quotes and that quote's exchange zone (mixed exchanges show the newest one's zone). North American zones read ET/CT/MT/PT; others the JDK short name; unknown zone reads UTC. Older than today shows "Fri 4:00 PM ET" or "Nov 13, 4:00 PM ET"; with clock skew the full date form is used so no wrong "today" is implied.
- **Error row vs stale row**: a row with a quote whose last refresh FAILED is an Error row (numbers dimmed, reason + Retry); merely old data is a Stale row (amber dot + age). A failed refresh therefore shows as an error even 20 s after a success, which is accurate but louder than a silent retry.
- **Rate-limit text** uses the repository's live `cooldownRemainingMs()` when set, else the error's retry-after; the text is recomputed whenever state changes (not on a timer), so the minute count can lag until the next state change.
- **Closed-market polling** is per symbol (60 s tick; due per `PollPolicy`), which keeps live symbols at one minute when markets are mixed.
- **Detail** polls nothing by itself: it fetches on open and on range change (the repository's `minRefetch` applies: 2 min for 5D, 30 min for longer); 1D is the list's own quote and is kept fresh by the controller. Price on the detail screen is the 1D quote's; change is that price against the selected range's reference close.
- **Replace** does not carry the old display name over (a name for a different instrument would be wrong).
- **Add sheet** stays open after a pick in add mode (so several symbols can be added), clears the field and shows "Added X"; Done, or any replace, closes it.
- Pausing cancels in-flight fetches (the repository restores any Loading state). Presenter edits run on `Dispatchers.IO`; the state flows are collected on the UI thread.
- LazyColumn rows are not keyed by symbol (a duplicate key would crash the screen; the store prevents duplicates but a UI must not depend on that).

## M1 audit fixes
Independent audit of a78e4ab; each claim was checked in code, fixed and covered by a regression test (mostly `AuditFixesTest`, `JvmHttpFetcherTest`).
1. **Fallback no longer wipes the row.** `MarketDataProvider.supports(symbol, range)`; Stooq serves 1D and mapped symbols only. A fallback result (not from the first provider) is merged onto the held quote: price, provider time, high, low and market state are taken, previous close, series, names are kept; with nothing held the fallback quote is used as is. A fallback quote more than 1 day older than the held one, or more than 1 day in the future, is rejected (counts as a failure). `Quote.source` records the provider (stamped by the repository when blank). A fallback sourced quote is never throttled by `minRefetch`, so the primary is retried on the next cycle. Caveat: after a session roll the kept previous close is one session old.
2. **Clocks.** `Clock.monoMs()` (injectable) drives ages, minRefetch and cooldowns; wall time only for the persisted savedAt and skew. A cache entry written "in the future" is Stale with unknown age (`QuoteStatus.Stale(ageKnown=false)`), never Fresh, and never blocks a refresh. `QuoteState.clockSkew` is set when the device clock is behind the provider time (or the cache write time).
3. **Watchlist power-cut safety.** Temp file written through `FileOutputStream`, `fd.sync()`, then atomic rename; `watchlist.bak` keeps the previous good copy; load order is main, then backup, then defaults, so a zero-length or truncated file recovers from the backup (`LoadStatus.RecoveredFromBackup`); a non-empty broken file is still kept as `watchlist.corrupt-<ts>.json`. `flushIfDirty()` retries a failed write. A newer-version file shows its readable items read-only and is never overwritten. Note the backup is the PREVIOUS copy, so recovering from it can lose the very last edit.
4. **Bounded fetches.** `withTimeoutOrNull(20 s)` around the whole fetch and `disconnect()` on cancellation (callers are released at once; a blocked DNS lookup cannot be interrupted but its thread ends with the lookup). No query2 retry when the failure says the device is offline (unknown host, no route, network unreachable). After the first Network or ClockOrTls failure (or two timeouts) the not-yet-started symbols of the same `refresh` call are failed at once.
5. **Out-of-order guard removed** (in-flight dedup already serialises a key); only the fallback sanity rule above remains.
6. **Currency quirks.** `Currencies.normalize`: `GBp` becomes `GBX`, `ZAc` becomes `ZAC`, `ILA` stays; other codes are upper-cased. Used by the Yahoo parser and the watchlist. `Currencies.isMinorUnit` / `majorOf` for the UI.
7. **Backoff.** One streak increment per cooldown window (4 concurrent 429s give a 30 s cooldown, not 240 s). A 5xx with a JSON error body is `Http(5xx)` and backs off. Stooq's 200 "Exceeded the daily hits limit" body is `RateLimited(1 h)`; Retry-After is honoured up to 1 h (`RepoConfig.maxRetryAfterMs`), exponential backoff stays capped at 15 min. New `ErrorKind.ClockOrTls` for TLS certificate date failures.
8. **Derived marketState** (only when Yahoo omits it) needs the device clock inside today's regular window AND a last trade at most 20 min old, else CLOSED; a device clock behind the last trade gives unknown (null). Holidays are only detected heuristically (a stale last trade reads as CLOSED).
9. **Quote additions**: `source`, `sessionStartMs`, `sessionEndMs`, `exchangeTimezoneName`, `priceHint` plus `Quote.decimals` (hint, else 4 for currency pairs, else 2). `QuoteRepository.forget(symbol)` drops states, cache entries and series for all ranges (and a fetch already running will not bring it back); `AppGraph.removeFromWatchlist` / `replaceInWatchlist` are the documented hooks for the UI.
10. `refreshing` is now a lock-guarded counter (stays true while any fetch is active). Stooq rows are parsed by header name and tolerate a missing trailing field. Tests added for plain-text 429, a response without a `timestamp` key and a `GBp` payload.

Decisions recorded: no `v8/spark` batch endpoint (unverifiable here; a future option for fewer requests). No TMX Money or Bank of Canada providers yet: see Next ideas.

## M1 decisions and judgement calls
- **JSON**: kotlinx.serialization-json 1.7.3 used through its `JsonElement` tree API with small defensive accessors (`provider/JsonSupport.kt`), no `@Serializable` classes and therefore no serialization compiler plugin (nothing to generate; wrong types and nulls in third-party JSON become null instead of exceptions). Only the runtime dependency was added, to both modules via the catalog.
- **Previous close**: D1 uses the previous SESSION close (`chartPreviousClose`, else `previousClose`); longer ranges use `chartPreviousClose` (close before the range start, so the change is the move over the displayed range), else the first series point. Stooq has no previous close (its Open is not one); a fallback result is merged onto the held quote (see audit fixes), but with nothing held the quote has `prevClose = null` and no series and the UI must tolerate that.
- **Currency is never guessed by providers.** Yahoo `currency` or blank; Stooq blank. The repository fills blank currency/exchange/name from the WatchItem (`hints`). Quick-add "add anyway" stores currency and exchange blank until the first good quote.
- **State keys**: `QuoteRepository.keyFor` is the plain symbol for D1 and `SYMBOL@5D` etc for other ranges, so a detail chart never overwrites the list row.
- **Cooldown is per provider, repository wide** (shared by all symbols, also applies to forced refreshes). 429 and 5xx trigger it (Retry-After honoured up to 1 h; else 30 s, 60 s... capped at 15 min; success resets; one failing window counts once). Network, timeout, 4xx and parse errors never trigger a cooldown (the user presses refresh when the hotspot returns).
- **Fallback rule**: any error moves to the next provider that `supports()` the symbol, which implements "NotFound is not retried on the fallback unless the mapping exists". The reported error is the first provider's.
- **Freshness is tracked by fetch time, not provider time** (provider `asOf` stops moving while a market is closed). Defaults: Stale after 5 min (1D), 15 min (5D), 6 h (longer); a non-forced refresh is skipped under 30 s (1D). Cache entries older than 7 days are discarded. `recomputeAges()` must be called periodically by the M2 refresh loop so Fresh turns Stale without a refresh.
- **Clocks**: in-process ages, minimum refetch and cooldowns use a MONOTONIC clock (`Clock.monoMs`); the wall clock is used only for persisted times (cache savedAt, log) and skew detection. Quote times always come from the provider.
- **WatchlistStore threading**: synchronous, lock-guarded file IO of a few hundred bytes; M2 should call it from `Dispatchers.IO`. Unknown JSON fields are ignored on read and not preserved on rewrite. A file with items that fail to parse is copied aside (not renamed) and the good items are kept.
- **Exchange display**: Yahoo's `fullExchangeName` is "Toronto" / "NasdaqGS", not "TSX" / "NASDAQ". `WatchItem.exchange` (defaults, or filled at add time) is only used when the quote has none, so M2 should decide which to show (suggest the WatchItem's for the two defaults).
- `JvmHttpFetcherTest` lives in `:sandbox` because `com.sun.net.httpserver` is not on the Android unit-test classpath; everything else is in `app/src/test`.
